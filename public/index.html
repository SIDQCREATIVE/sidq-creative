Frequently asked questions

What is a clipping agency?

We turn long videos such as podcasts and streams into short clips and help them reach more viewers.

What is the difference between private and public clipping?

Private clipping uses a small dedicated team that works only on your content. Public clipping uses a large open network of clippers who post your clips on many pages.

What do I need to send?

Just your raw long video. No prep is needed.

Do you write hooks, titles and captions?

Yes. Our short-form editing plus content writing service includes them.
